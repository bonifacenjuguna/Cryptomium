How alerts work

Every coin has a step. For most coins it is a percentage, for example 0.5% for Bitcoin. When the price has moved one full step from the last alert, in either direction, {{BRAND}} posts a banner to {{CHANNEL_HANDLE}} showing the coin, the price and whether it rose or fell.

If the market jumps several steps at once, you get one alert for the furthest level reached, not a burst of messages.

Stablecoins (USDT and USDC) work differently. They only alert when the price strays from $1.00 by more than a small safety band, so a quiet day means no messages.

Banners are posted by {{BOT_HANDLE}}. It only posts to the channel, so there is nothing to message it about.

Where the numbers come from

Prices come from major public market data providers and exchanges, and refresh every few seconds while you are on the page. Market cap, volume and history come from aggregated market data and update every few minutes.

Different exchanges quote slightly different prices at any moment, so figures here can differ a little from the one you trade on.

Frequently asked questions

Is {{BRAND}} free?

Yes. The website and the Telegram channel are free.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Starred coins and your theme and currency choices are saved in your own browser.

How do I get the alerts?

Join {{CHANNEL_HANDLE}} on Telegram and turn notifications on for the channel. Every alert arrives as a single banner.

Which currencies can I view prices in?

US dollars by default. Use the currency menu at the top of the page to switch, and your choice is remembered. Alerts in the channel are always in US dollars.

Why does a price differ from my exchange?

Exchanges set their own prices, so small differences are normal, especially for less traded coins and during fast moves.

Is this financial advice?

No. {{BRAND}} shows market information only. Do your own research before making decisions with your money.
