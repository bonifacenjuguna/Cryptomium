Update mode

How updates installAutomatic checks now and then, downloads a new version quietly, and switches to it when you open the app or put it away, never in the middle of what you are doing.

Offline and saved data

The app opens without a connection. Prices, charts and news you have already seen are kept so a dropped signal never blanks a screen. They are labelled with their age until live data returns.
