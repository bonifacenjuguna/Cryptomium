Privacy

{{BRAND}} has no accounts and no ads, and it does not track you.

Kept only on your device

Your theme and layout choices, currency, starred coins, portfolio holdings and price alerts are saved in your own browser or app. They are not sent to us, unless you turn on notifications (below).

If you turn on notifications

So an alert can reach you with the app closed, {{BRAND}} stores on its server: your device's push address and encryption keys (given by your browser, not your identity), a random device code, the price alerts you set (coin, type, target) and your milestone choice. There is no name, email, phone number or location. Notifications are delivered through your platform's push service (Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft), which sees only an encrypted message.

Turning notifications off in Settings, Price alerts deletes all of this at once. A device that stays unused for four months is deleted automatically, and so is one that can no longer receive messages.

What the site's server sees

Like any website, the price service sees your IP address in its routine request logs and uses it only to limit abuse. Prices, news and charts come from public market-data providers; they are requested by our server, not by your browser.

Questions or deletion

Everything on your device can be cleared in Settings, Data and privacy. For anything else, message {{CHANNEL_HANDLE}} on Telegram.
